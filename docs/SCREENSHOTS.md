# Screenshot and GIF placeholders

The preview SVGs in this folder are stand-ins for real project captures. Replace them when recording a configured LORE instance:

- `overlay-preview.svg`: replace with an OBS overlay screenshot.
- `archive-preview.svg`: replace with a screenshot of the LORE archive.
- `control-preview.svg`: replace with a screenshot of the streamer control room.
- `ritual-demo.gif`: capture a summon through canonization and save the GIF at this path.

Capture at 1920x1080 for the overlay. Hide access tokens and other private account information before sharing screenshots.
